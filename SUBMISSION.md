# WALLSIGHT — Round 1 Submission Package

## Title
**WallSight — The House That Sees Without Watching: Device-Free Elder & Child Safety via WiFi-CSI Ambient Sensing**

## Project description (for the form / pitch)
> WallSight turns the WiFi signals already present in every home into a silent guardian. Using Channel State Information (CSI) — the fine-grained radio measurements of ordinary WiFi — small plug-and-play boxes (~₹400 each) detect a person's presence, movement, and breathing **through walls**, with **no cameras, no microphones, no wearables, and nothing required from the person being monitored**. A cloud engine fuses the boxes' signals with Bluetooth and phone signals into a confidence-weighted picture of which room is occupied, tracks movement room to room, and alerts a caregiver's phone when a toddler crosses the kitchen door at 2 AM, a dementia patient wanders out of a safe zone, or an elderly parent's breathing stops. The hardware stays dumb and cheap; intelligence lives in the cloud behind a subscription — Starter (₹199/mo) to Family (₹499/mo). No device on the market senses the home this way without the person participating — WallSight requires nothing but the WiFi that's already there.

# WALLSIGHT — Round 1 Submission Package

## Title
**WallSight — The House That Sees Without Watching: Device-Free Elder & Child Safety via WiFi-CSI Ambient Sensing**

## Project description (for the form / pitch)
> WallSight turns the WiFi signals already present in every home into a silent guardian. Using Channel State Information (CSI) — the fine-grained radio measurements of ordinary WiFi — three plug-and-play boxes (~₹500 each) detect a person's presence, movement, and breathing **through walls**, with **no cameras, no microphones, no wearables, and nothing required from the person being monitored**. A 4-step setup wizard draws the room, places the boxes, and shows an **honest capability map** — what the boxes can sense, where walls block, where the blind spots are. The operator then draws **zones** (Inner / Outer / Danger / Custom) and sets per-zone rules: allowed hours, max dwell time, alert severity, night-mode. A zone policy engine watches dwell timers and transitions — *Granny left the bed at 2 AM and lingered 12 minutes in the kitchen zone* → hard alert on the caregiver dashboard. Breathing and heart rate come from the proven open sensing stack (MIT, attributed); the care product — wizard, zones, policies, dashboard, consent-based BLE identity, multi-tenant API — is built by us. Hardware stays dumb and cheap; intelligence lives in the cloud behind a subscription (Starter ₹199/mo to Family ₹499/mo). No device on the market senses the home this way without the person participating — WallSight requires nothing but the WiFi that's already there.
